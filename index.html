How much of the whale short is a bet?
I track ~4,000 large Hyperliquid wallets every 15 minutes and strip out market makers, vaults and hedged shorts. In HYPE, most of the short side turns out to be staking hedges.

Do Hyperliquid longs still pay more than Binance longs?
I compared three and a half years of hourly funding on Hyperliquid, Binance and Bybit. The gap averaged about 6.5% a year, shrank to 1 to 5% in 2026, and now earns about what T-bills pay once you hold both legs.

How much of the bitcoin ETF bid is a basis trade?
I matched weekly US spot bitcoin ETF flows against hedge-fund positions in CME futures, with the hypotheses fixed before the test. For each BTC of inflow in 2025-26, leveraged funds added 0.23 BTC of short. A falling futures premium did not predict outflows.

Does a big week of stablecoin issuance predict bitcoin's next week?
I tested the idea that heavy USDT and USDC minting comes before bitcoin rallies, with the threshold fixed on 2021-23 and judged on 2024-26. It failed: in the heaviest issuance weeks bitcoin fell 1.7% on average the week after. Issuance follows price instead.
