How much of the whale short is a bet?
I track ~4,000 large Hyperliquid wallets every 15 minutes and strip out market makers, vaults and hedged shorts. In HYPE, most of the short side turns out to be staking hedges.

Do Hyperliquid longs still pay more than Binance longs?
I compared three and a half years of hourly funding on Hyperliquid, Binance and Bybit. The gap averaged about 6.5% a year, shrank to 1 to 5% in 2026, and now earns about what T-bills pay once you hold both legs.

When do Hyperliquid liquidations cascade?
I record trade flow, open interest, funding, book depth and a sample of liquidations on BTC, ETH, SOL and HYPE, then test what comes before a cascade and how much of the move comes back.
